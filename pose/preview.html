Front views are authored for the six exercises where the frontal plane carries something the side view physically cannot: stance and grip width, elbow flare, knee tracking, and which hand is throwing. Deadlift, RDL, row, bench and the kettlebell swing are sagittal movements whose front view is either a person standing still or a foreshortened hinge this rig cannot honestly draw, so they stay single-view rather than showing you a panel that teaches nothing.

Pause, then drag the slider to step through any position. Bar path traces where the bar travels — on a squat and deadlift it should be a straight vertical line.

Every position is solved from anatomically-scaled segments with the feet planted, and checked programmatically across the whole continuous motion: limb lengths never stretch, planted feet never slide, nothing passes through the floor, and the bar path stays vertical. Nothing here is generated imagery.
