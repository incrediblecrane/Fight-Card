Meal planner: what is a “planned meal”?

Click through this and tell me where it feels wrong. Nothing here is saved and nothing touches the real app.

The question. Today a recipe carries two fields: inPlan (a tick) and day (one weekday). So a recipe is planned or it isn’t, and it sits on at most one day.

The alternative is that a planned meal becomes its own thing — {recipe, date, slot, portions} — and a recipe is just a recipe. Same recipe on three days is three planned meals.

Everything below runs on both models at once, so you can watch where the current one runs out of room. Switch between them with the two buttons.
